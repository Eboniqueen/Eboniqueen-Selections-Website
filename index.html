OUR STORY

From a small idea to a collection with purpose.

Eboniqueen Selections was registered in July 2026, but the story began much earlier. The business was formerly Kitty Galourz, selling mainly feminine-care products and soap bars in Portmore and Kingston while I worked as a filing clerk.

After COVID-19, I began selling products for extra income. But the dream was always bigger: I wanted to create. As a child, I mixed cornmeal and aloe vera into homemade scrubs while everyone laughed and asked what “concoction” I was making. I loved art, experimenting and bringing ideas to life.

Rebranding gave me a name that felt intimate and meaningful. Eboniqueen — Black Queen — became a reminder of the royalty, value and potential I believe every person carries.

I expanded into products I could create myself: body butters, body scrubs, hair growth elixir, soap bars and books, with body oils and candles coming soon. Premium ingredients, rich textures and a spa-like experience are central to the collection.

Feminine care became part of the journey after I supported an online business in that space and experienced how those products could affect confidence. I wanted to create a place where women could feel cared for, informed and comfortable.

During nursing school, I developed the strategy that became Fundamental Nursing Terms & Journal. I built it page by page and grew to love the process. I used Christ as a foundation for reassurance and to help connect learning with purpose.

The Inner You runs deep. I believe there is a version of ourselves we want to unlock but sometimes do not know how to reach. Through self-care, our thoughts and our daily practices, we can begin to unleash that part of ourselves.

Even as a mother of two, these ideas continued to flow. Fear and doubt have made me stop focusing on myself at times, and I have almost given up. What kept me going was my family and the belief that God did not give me a spirit of fear. I held on to the vision, strength and second chance.

“Listen to your body, know your limits, and rise to the occasion.”

My bigger vision is to grow Eboniqueen into a holistic space for rejuvenation of the mind, body and soul — beginning in Jamaica and one day reaching beyond our shores.

SKIN EDUCATION

Why exfoliating and moisturizing matter

Exfoliating helps lift away built-up dead skin from the surface so skin feels smoother. Gentle exfoliation can be a useful part of a body-care routine when it suits your skin.

Moisturizing helps support the skin barrier and reduce the uncomfortable feeling of dryness. After exfoliating, applying a body butter helps replenish and soften the skin.

How to care for your body butter in Jamaica

Keep it in a cool, dry place away from sunlight and excessive heat. Some customers may refrigerate it during very hot weather.

Because the butter is whipped with air, heat can cause it to soften or melt. If that happens, it can return to a firmer state when cooled. The texture may change or feel slightly grainy, but melting by itself does not mean the product is spoiled.

Yes. Heat can soften or melt whipped body butter. Store it in a cool, dry place away from sunlight. Melting does not automatically mean the product is spoiled.

Changes in temperature can affect texture. A slightly grainy texture does not automatically mean the product has spoiled.
