OUR STORY

A collection built from creativity, purpose and faith.

Eboniqueen Selections grew from an idea I had carried for years. The business was formerly Kitty Galourz, selling mainly feminine-care products and soap bars in Portmore and Kingston.

After COVID-19, I began selling products for extra income. As a child, I mixed cornmeal and aloe vera into homemade scrubs while everyone laughed and asked what “concoction” I was making. I loved art, experimenting and bringing ideas to life.

I began making my own body butters about two and a half years ago. That creative journey became one of the foundations of Eboniqueen.

Eboniqueen — Black Queen — became a reminder of the royalty, value and potential I believe every person carries. “Selections” reflects how intentional I am about what belongs in the collection.

Our products use premium ingredients and are designed to give a spa-like experience. Whipped butters melt upon contact with the skin, a little goes a long way, and our rich foaming scrubs turn everyday care into a sensory ritual. Jamaican names add a little piece of paradise.

During nursing school, I developed Fundamental Nursing Terms & Journal page by page, using Christ as a foundation for reassurance and purpose.

The Inner You runs deep. I believe there is a version of ourselves we want to unlock. Through self-care, our thoughts and daily practices, we can begin to unleash that part of ourselves.

Fear and doubt have made me stop focusing on myself at times, and I have almost given up. What kept me going was my family and the belief that God did not give me a spirit of fear. I held on to the vision, strength and second chance.

“Listen to your body, know your limits, and rise to the occasion.”

My bigger vision is to grow Eboniqueen into a holistic space for rejuvenation of the mind, body and soul — beginning in Jamaica and one day reaching beyond our shores.

THE INNER YOU · SKIN EDUCATION

Understand your skin. Build your ritual.

1. Why Exfoliating Matters

Gentle exfoliation helps remove accumulated dead skin from the surface so skin can feel smoother.

2. Why Moisturizing Matters

Moisturizing helps support the skin barrier and reduce the uncomfortable feeling of dryness.

3. Exfoliate → Moisturize: The Perfect Pair

Gently exfoliate, rinse, pat dry and apply body butter while skin is slightly damp.

4. Body Butter vs. Lotion

Body butters are generally richer than many lotions and are especially appealing for people who prefer richer care for dry-feeling skin.

5. Why Does Eboniqueen Body Butter Melt?

Heat can soften or melt whipped body butter. Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Some customers may refrigerate it during very hot weather.

6. Why Does My Body Butter Feel Grainy?

Temperature changes can affect the texture of butter-based products. A texture change does not automatically mean the product is spoiled.

7. A Little Goes a Long Way

Scoop a small amount, warm it between your palms and massage it into the skin. Start small and add more if needed.

When Should I Exfoliate?

Exfoliation does not need to happen every day. Start gently and adjust according to how your skin responds. Avoid exfoliating irritated, broken or freshly shaved skin.

Patch Test: Always patch test a small area of your hand or inner arm before using a new product. Discontinue use if irritation or an allergic reaction occurs.
